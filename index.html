We build it. You own it.

We are a small Oakland studio, and we started Merritt Digital because the web got expensive for the people who need it most. A shop, a garage, a restaurant, a one-person trade — all of them get quoted a monthly fee forever, and half of them do not really own the site they are paying for. So we do it the other way round. We build yours, hand you the keys, and step back: the files, the domain and the hosting all in your name, with nothing left switched on at our end. If you come back to us later, it is because the work earned it, not because a contract says you have to.

What that looks like

A capability you can’t inspect is just a claim.

So this section audits the page you are standing on, on your device, on this load. Open the developer tools and check any line of it.

Dimension
1,193 frames · not a video
The film behind this page is a sequence of still frames, drawn to a canvas and indexed by how far you have scrolled. That is why it runs backwards the instant you scroll up, and why there is no audio to mute. You see the whole frame, uncropped, at every screen size.
Frame
Readable before any of it loads
The first still is part of the page itself, so you can read every word and reach the prices before a single frame of the film has arrived. The rest streams in behind it, coarse first and then sharper. If your connection gives out halfway, the page is still right — you simply keep the still.

Libre Caslon Display for the things of value, Archivo for the apparatus, self-hosted and subset to only the glyphs this page sets. No font network, no tracking.

We open your site on a phone

Before anything is designed, we look at what you have the way your customers do: on a phone, on a bad connection, in daylight. What’s slow, what’s unreadable and what’s missing gets written down first.

We find the object you already own

Every business has one thing its customers already trust: the packaging, the certificate, the storefront, the logo that hasn’t changed in thirty years. We name what makes that object work, and the site becomes it. That is why our sites don’t look like each other.

We build the real thing

Not a mockup and not a theme. One page of hand-written code, no framework, with one element carrying real live data, composed three separate times for phone, tablet and desktop. Everything moving on this page was built the same way.

We hand you the keys

It goes live at an address you can open, share and sleep on — and from that moment it is yours. The files, the domain and the hosting account are all set up in your name, so there is nothing we can switch off and nothing you have to keep paying us to keep.
