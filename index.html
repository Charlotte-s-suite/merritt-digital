The work

Three sites. Three objects.

We don’t have a house style; we have a house method. For each of these, we found the object the client’s customers already trust, and built the site as that object. You can open every one of them right now.

Specimen No. 01

Fort Knox Rare Coin & Bullion

Alameda, California · precious metals, by appointment

The site IS the PAMP assay card.

Their customers already trust one object: the sealed assay card a bullion bar ships inside. We took its grammar whole: the paper presentation window floated on a dark field, the engraved filigree cropped at the edge, the letterspaced serif nameplate, the hallmark punches. The site became that card. The spot prices in the window are a real feed, and they fail soft: when it doesn’t answer, the page says so rather than show you a broken number.

Specimen No. 02

Oud-Kempen Eetcafé

Stavenisse, Zeeland · restaurant, rentals, holiday home

The site IS the golden-hour nameplate plate.

The café already owned the answer: their own logo, an espresso serif nameplate on parchment with a brass flourish. That became the whole system: framed golden-hour plates on a parchment field, brass foil rules, the flourish reused as the section divider. Their extra services don’t hide in a menu: table, bike, jacuzzi and holiday-home bookings sit on the front page as cards, in Dutch, English and German.

Specimen No. 03

Toy Safari

Alameda, California · independent toy store

The site IS the store walkthrough.

A toy store’s magic is the walk through it, so the site is that walk: fourteen habitats rather than a grid of part numbers, in the store’s own zebra-and-jungle palette. Search speaks the way people actually ask for toys (by character, by franchise, by age, by scale), and the gift finder sits where the balloon counter does. The point was never a catalogue; it was making the room navigable from a phone.

The method

One afternoon, four moves.

The same sequence every time. It is why a free demo is possible at all: the work is disciplined, not heroic.

Audit

We open your current site on a phone first, the way your customers do. What’s slow, what’s unreadable, what’s missing gets written down before anything gets designed.

On your phone, not ours

Extract

We find the object your customers already trust: the packaging, the certificate, the storefront, the logo you’ve had for thirty years. Then we name its grammar, and the site becomes that object.

Yours, not your category’s

Build

One file, no framework, no build step, which is why it loads instantly. One element on the page carries real live data. Every layout is composed three times, for phone, tablet and desktop.

Deploy

It goes to a real HTTPS address the same day, with a printed pitch to keep. You open it, share it, sleep on it. Nothing is owed for any of that.
