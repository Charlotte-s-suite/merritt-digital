You own the site outright. The design, the files, the domain and the hosting account are all in your name, and we hand the whole thing over. It belongs to you the way your sign or your van belongs to you.

Anything after launch

Support is not a plan. There is nothing to sign up for, no retainer and no monthly minimum, so there is nothing to cancel. If you want changes later, we handle it case by case, as and when you ask.

You are billed for the time it takes. We agree the work with you first, quote it in writing before starting, and invoice once it is finished. You are never charged for a month in which you asked for nothing.

Your site does not depend on us. Because you own it and host it in your own name, choosing to do nothing costs nothing and breaks nothing.

Why there is no monthly fee

Most small-business websites are built once and then billed forever. We do it the other way round: your website, your hosting account and your domain name are set up in your name, and we hand you the files. There is nothing for us to switch off, so there is nothing to charge you rent for.

That means we only earn again if you decide the work was worth coming back for. We prefer it that way.

What happens when

  • Nothing to pay to find out. Tell us about your business and we will talk through what you need before any money changes hands.
  • The initial design fee covers the build, including reasonable adjustments to get it right before handover.
  • Anything after that is quoted case by case and agreed with you in writing before we start.
  • Hosting and domain costs are paid by you, directly, to whichever company you choose. For a small business site this is often free or a few dollars a month, and it is never paid to us.
